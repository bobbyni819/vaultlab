# Draft introduction — spatial transcriptomics in the lung

The first wave of spatial transcriptomics methods established 55-µm
spot resolution (Asp et al., 2020), opening systematic study of cellular
geography across many tissues.

Foundational follow-up work in tumour ecosystems (Park et al., 2023)
showed that infected niches differ from healthy niches in cell-type
composition, with the difference reproducible across donors.

A methods paper (DOI: 10.1038/s41592-024-02123-w) introduced an
open-source neighborhood metric used widely downstream.

Reviewers also flagged the in-press Doe paper claiming superhuman
performance (Doe et al., 2099) — note the year — and the original
sequencing paper at PMID: 28104796.

Two more recent applications further demonstrate spatial methods in
lung: (Garcia and Singh, 2024) and the SHG-imaging extension
(Pentimalli and Rajewsky, 2025) at doi:10.1016/j.cels.2025.101261.

Lee et al. (2021) reviewed the broader landscape; a deeper methods
treatment is in their textbook chapter (Lee 1822) — the apparent
century-old year is intentional for this demo and should be flagged.
